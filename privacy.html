PRIVACY POLICY · V0.5.0

Your tokens stay on your phone.

BotDroid is built so that your bot tokens, LLM API keys, and message contents never sit on our servers — because we don't operate any. This page explains exactly what lives on your device, what gets sent where, and what you control.

LAST UPDATED · 2026-07-25 · EFFECTIVE · 2026-05-27

TL;DR

  • BotDroid runs entirely on your Android device. We do not operate a backend server that handles your bot traffic or messages.
  • Bot tokens and LLM API keys are stored in encrypted local storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences, AES-256-GCM with a key sealed by the Android Keystore).
  • Outbound network calls go only to: (a) the messaging providers you configure (Telegram, Discord, Slack, Zalo, etc.), (b) the LLM provider you point at with your API key, (c) Google Firebase for crash reporting and app configuration, and (d) Google Play Billing for subscription purchases.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all your local data, including tokens and message history. There is no separate "delete account" flow because there is no account.

1 · WHO WE ARE

"BotDroid" (the "app", "we", "us") is published independently. Contact for privacy questions: kaiteam@proton.me. We are the data controller for the limited diagnostic and purchase data described below; for everything you configure inside the app (bot tokens, LLM keys, messages), you remain the controller — we never see it.

2 · WHAT THIS POLICY COVERS

This policy applies to the BotDroid Android application distributed on Google Play and to this marketing website (botdroid.app). It does not cover the third-party services you choose to connect to from inside BotDroid — those services (Telegram, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Anthropic via OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, DeepSeek, xAI, Cloudflare Workers AI, Discord, Slack, Zalo, LINE, DingTalk, Feishu, WeCom, etc.) have their own privacy policies, which you should read separately.

3 · DATA WE HANDLE

3.1 — Stored only on your device (we never see it)

  • Bot tokens for Telegram, Discord, Slack, Zalo, LINE, DingTalk, Feishu, and WeCom that you paste into the app.
  • LLM API keys for OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, DeepSeek, xAI, Cloudflare Workers AI, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure.
  • Webhook URLs and channel secrets you enter (e.g. Zalo webhook, LINE channel secret).
  • Bot scripts (Python scripts you import or write inside the app) and per-bot configuration.
  • Live logs emitted by your bots while they run on your phone.
  • Scheduled task definitions (prompts, intervals, target chat IDs).
  • Per-bot usage metrics (token counts, estimated cost, latency) computed locally for the in-app dashboard.

Secrets in this category are stored in Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences using AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is generated on-device and held in the Android Keystore, which is hardware-backed on devices that support it. Scripts and logs are stored in the app's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device.

None of this category is transmitted to BotDroid's developers or to any backend we operate. It is transmitted only when you trigger a bot run — at which point it goes directly from your phone to the third-party service you configured (e.g., Telegram's servers, your chosen LLM provider).

3.2 — Sent to third parties at your direction

  • Messaging providers (Telegram, Discord, Slack, Zalo, …): the app long-polls or connects to each provider you configure, using your token. Message contents flow between your phone and the provider's API. We do not proxy this traffic.
  • LLM providers (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, DeepSeek, xAI, Cloudflare, custom OpenAI-compatible endpoints): when a bot calls a model, the app issues an HTTPS request from your phone directly to the API base URL you set, using your API key. The request body (prompts, conversation history) is visible to that provider per its own privacy policy. We do not proxy this traffic.
  • picoclaw agent (bundled binary): when you use the Picobot / agent mode, an embedded Go binary executes on your phone and calls the LLM provider directly with your key. It does not phone home to BotDroid.

3.3 — Diagnostic and configuration data (Firebase)

BotDroid integrates Google Firebase to keep the app stable and current. Specifically:

  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash stack traces, device model, OS version, app version, a stable installation ID, and breadcrumb logs we emit (which contain operational events like "bot started", "schedule fired", "LLM call failed: HTTP 401" — never your tokens, keys, message contents, or LLM prompt/response bodies).
  • Firebase Analytics: standard Google Analytics for Firebase events (app open, screen view, button tap on key flows) plus a small number of custom events we use to understand which features are working (e.g. bot_started, llm_test_succeeded, upgrade_button_tapped). No event carries your tokens, keys, or message contents.
  • Firebase Remote Config: the app fetches the curated LLM-provider/model catalog (llm_catalog_json) and similar configuration so we can update available models without shipping a new app build. This is a one-way fetch of public configuration; no personal data flows back beyond the standard Firebase request metadata.
  • Firebase Realtime Database: used to track redemptions of promo/trial codes, but only if you choose to redeem one in Settings. The email or code you enter is SHA-256 hashed on your device before anything leaves your phone — we store only that hash, your Firebase installation ID, the redemption timestamp, and the trial length granted, so a capped promotion can't be redeemed twice from the same install. The raw email or code you typed is never transmitted or stored anywhere.

Firebase processes the above as a sub-processor on our behalf. See Google's policy at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

3.4 — Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you upgrade to BotDroid Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive a purchase token and product ID from Google to verify your entitlement; we do not receive your full name, email, postal address, or payment instrument. Google's policy applies to the payment itself: policies.google.com/privacy.

3.5 — Marketing website (botdroid.app)

This site is static and does not set tracking cookies of its own. It loads webfonts from Google Fonts and Tailwind from a CDN; those providers see standard request metadata (IP address, user-agent) per their own policies. We do not run analytics on this site at the moment; if that changes, this policy will be updated.

4 · ANDROID PERMISSIONS WE REQUEST AND WHY

  • INTERNET — to talk to the messaging and LLM providers you configure, and to Firebase.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE — the core promise of the app is running bots 24/7. A foreground service keeps your bot process alive in the background and shows the persistent "BotDroid is running" notification required by Android.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — to display the foreground-service notification and, optionally, alerts about bot crashes or scheduled events.
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS — Android aggressively kills background work on most OEMs. We ask for this exemption so scheduled triggers and long-running bots actually survive overnight. The prompt is shown only when you tap "Enable" in-app; you can decline.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore your scheduled bots after a phone reboot.
  • WAKE_LOCK — to keep the CPU awake while a bot is mid-call, so a long LLM response doesn't get suspended.

5 · DATA RETENTION

  • On-device data (tokens, scripts, logs, schedules, metrics) — retained until you delete the bot, clear app data, or uninstall the app. We have no remote copy to retain or delete on your behalf.
  • Crashlytics crash reports — retained by Google per Firebase's retention policy (currently up to 90 days for individual crash details, 6 months for aggregated trends).
  • Analytics events — retained for the period you can configure in your Firebase Analytics user-data-retention setting (default: 2 months for user-level data, 14 months for event-level).
  • Purchase records — retained by Google Play; we retain only the minimal entitlement state (Free / Pro) needed to unlock features.

6 · YOUR CHOICES AND RIGHTS

  • Delete everything: uninstalling BotDroid removes all on-device data instantly. There is no separate account to close.
  • Revoke a token: delete it from inside the relevant provider (e.g. Telegram BotFather) — the local copy in BotDroid becomes inert.
  • Opt out of Crashlytics / Analytics: email kaiteam@proton.me. We expect to add an in-app toggle in a future release; until then we will handle opt-outs manually on request.
  • Access / correct / port: because everything personal lives on your device, you already have full access. For diagnostic data Firebase holds on our behalf, contact us and we will request export or deletion from Google.
  • Regional rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / LGPD): you may exercise any rights granted by the law of your residence by emailing kaiteam@proton.me. We aim to respond within 30 days. Legal basis under GDPR is (i) contract for entitlement processing, (ii) legitimate interest for crash diagnostics and product analytics, and (iii) consent for any future opt-in features.

7 · SECURITY

  • All API traffic between the app and third-party providers uses HTTPS.
  • Tokens and API keys at rest are encrypted with AES-256-GCM via EncryptedSharedPreferences; the unwrap key is held in the Android Keystore.
  • Bot scripts and the SQLite database are stored in the app's private storage. They are excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup (backup_rules.xml / data_extraction_rules.xml) so tokens are never copied off-device by Auto Backup.
  • Release builds are signed with a developer-controlled key and minified/shrunk by R8.
  • BotDroid does not phone home with your tokens, keys, or message contents — there is no backend that could be breached to leak them. The compromise surface is your phone and the third-party providers you've chosen to use.

8 · CHILDREN

BotDroid is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will work with you to remove any related diagnostic data.

9 · INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Crash and analytics data processed by Firebase may be transferred to and stored in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Google's standard contractual clauses and adequacy frameworks apply; see Google's privacy policy for current details.
