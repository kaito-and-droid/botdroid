import { Card, CardGrid, Steps, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

The **Offline Assistant** template, and the **On-Device** provider, generate replies on your phone. No cloud AI, no key. The bot still needs internet to receive and send chat messages.

## Pick a model

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Qwen2.5 0.5B" icon="rocket">
		**4 GB RAM or more** · about 550 MB download

		Faster replies. Good for short answers and quick rewrites.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Qwen2.5 1.5B" icon="star">
		**6 GB RAM or more** · about 1.6 GB download

		Better answers, a bit slower.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

The app only offers models your phone can run.

## Set it up

<Steps>
1. Tap **Add bot** and choose **Offline Assistant**.
2. Pick a model and download it once, on Wi-Fi.
3. Paste your Telegram bot token and tap **Start**.
</Steps>

<Aside>
Small models are great for chat and rewriting but less accurate than big cloud models. Replies use CPU and battery, so keep the phone charging if it runs all day.
</Aside>
