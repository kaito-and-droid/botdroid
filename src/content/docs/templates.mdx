Templates are the fastest way to a working bot. Tap **Add bot**, pick one, fill in its settings, and start it. You can change everything later on the bot's **Config** tab.

- **Python script** templates run on the phone and work in a messenger such as Telegram or from [Chat](/guide/chat/) with `/`. Most need no API key.
- **AI agent** templates need an [AI provider](/setup/ai-providers/) key (or an on-device model).
- **MCP** templates come with a [tool server](/setup/mcp-tools/) attached and are marked BETA in the app.

Some templates are only available with Pro. The app shows a lock on those.
