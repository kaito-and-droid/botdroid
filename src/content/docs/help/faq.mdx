Tap a question to open it.

<div class="faq">

<details>
<summary>Does my phone need to stay on?</summary>

Yes. Your phone is the server, so bots are online while it's on, connected, and allowed to run BotDroid in the background. A spare phone on a charger makes a great bot host.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Where are my tokens and API keys stored?</summary>

On your phone, encrypted per bot with AES-256-GCM in the Android Keystore. They're never logged, only sent to the service they belong to, and never put in backup files.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Does BotDroid read my messages?</summary>

No. Messages go between the chat app and your phone, and for AI bots to the provider you chose. On-device bots don't send message text to any AI service. {/* ASSISTANT:start */}Assistant messages in Chat may use a cloud backup unless you turn it off in **Settings**; no BotDroid account is needed.{/* ASSISTANT:end */} See the [privacy policy](/privacy).
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do I need Telegram or another messenger?</summary>

For messenger use, yes: you connect a bot to a messenger such as Telegram. Chat in the app works without one. [Chat →](/guide/chat/)
</details>

{/* ASSISTANT:start */}
<details>
<summary>What is the Assistant?</summary>

The Assistant answers when you type a plain message in Chat. It needs no setup, replies on your phone first, and can use an optional cloud backup within a daily limit. It is text only.
</details>
{/* ASSISTANT:end */}

<details>
<summary>Do I need to know how to code?</summary>

No. Templates have forms for their settings. Python scripts are there if you want them.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which phones are supported?</summary>

Android phones with a 64-bit ARM chip (arm64-v8a), which covers almost all recent phones. On-device AI needs 4 GB of RAM or more.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which AI providers work? Can I use Claude?</summary>

OpenRouter, OpenAI, Gemini, Groq, NVIDIA, DeepSeek, xAI, Cloudflare Workers AI, and any OpenAI-compatible endpoint. Claude works through OpenRouter. [AI providers →](/setup/ai-providers/)
</details>

<details>
<summary>Who pays for the AI?</summary>

For your AI bots, you pay your provider directly with your own key. Several have free tiers or free models. On-device bots cost nothing to run. {/* ASSISTANT:start */}The Assistant in Chat needs no key and no BotDroid account.{/* ASSISTANT:end */}
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can one bot answer on Telegram and Discord at the same time?</summary>

Yes. AI agent bots can connect several channels. [Managing bots →](/guide/managing-bots/)
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I move my bots to a new phone?</summary>

**Settings → Backup & Restore**: export, then import on the new phone. Re-enter tokens and keys afterwards, because backups never contain secrets.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is BotDroid open source?</summary>

The AI agent runtime is the open-source [PicoClaw](https://github.com/sipeed/picoclaw). The BotDroid app itself is not open source.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does Pro include?</summary>

Some templates, extra bots and channels, and scheduled tasks need Pro. The app marks those features and shows current plans through Google Play.

In Chat, Python bots {/* ASSISTANT:start */}and the Assistant {/* ASSISTANT:end */}are free; AI bots need Pro.
</details>

</div>
