About **5 minutes** from install to a bot that answers. Start with a Telegram template: it has the fewest steps.

<Steps>

1. **Install BotDroid**

   Any Android phone with a 64-bit (ARM64) chip, which covers almost every recent phone.

   <LinkButton href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.kaito.botdroid" icon="external" target="_blank">Get it on Google Play</LinkButton>

2. **Allow background running**

   When onboarding asks, exempt BotDroid from battery optimization. On Xiaomi, Samsung, Oppo, Vivo or Huawei, also allow **OEM Autostart** in Settings. [Checklist →](/guide/keep-bots-running/)

3. **Tap Add bot and pick a template**

   Try **Weather Bot** (no AI key) or **Q&A Assistant** (AI). [Browse all 26 →](/templates/)

4. **Connect a messenger and paste its token**

   Every bot needs one messenger channel, such as Telegram, Zalo or WhatsApp. Telegram: open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather), send `/newbot`, copy the token. [Other apps →](/setup/channels/)

5. **AI bots: add a provider key**

   Pick a provider, paste your key, choose a model. Or pick **On-Device** for no key at all. [Providers →](/setup/ai-providers/)

6. **Save and tap Start** 🎉

   Send your bot a message from Telegram, or from **Chat** in the app. Nothing back? Open **View logs** on the bot's Overview tab.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Try it before you make a bot">
Open **Chat** from Home to talk to the Assistant or run a template command such as `/weather Hanoi`, with no bot and no token. [Chat guide →](/guide/chat/)
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="No reply?">
The [troubleshooter](/help/troubleshooting/) finds the fix in two clicks.
</Aside>
