Android and phone makers stop background apps to save battery. Tick these off once and your bots stay online. Your progress is saved in this browser.

	{ title: 'Set background usage to Unrestricted', text: 'Android Settings → Apps → BotDroid → Battery → Unrestricted.' },

<Aside type="tip" title="Dedicated bot phone?">
Keep it on a charger and on Wi-Fi. BotDroid restarts your bots after a reboot.
