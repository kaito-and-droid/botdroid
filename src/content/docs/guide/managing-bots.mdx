import { Tabs, TabItem, Aside } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

Tap a bot to open it. A bot's card and Bot Detail both have a **Chat** shortcut that opens [Chat](/guide/chat/) with that bot pre-addressed. The tabs below match the tabs in the app.

<Tabs>
	<TabItem label="Overview" icon="analytics">
		Status, uptime, restarts and **execution mode**.

		- **Start** / **Stop** the bot
		- **View logs** to see what it's doing
		- Turn on a notification for when it crashes and won't restart
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Channels" icon="comment-alt">
		Every connected chat app with its status. Tap one to edit it, or **Add channel** to connect another ([get tokens](/setup/channels/)).

		- One channel is the **primary**. Change it with **Make primary**.
		- The primary or the only channel can't be removed.
		- Changes on a running bot restart it automatically.

		Python and on-device bots use one Telegram channel.

		A bot also works in the in-app Chat without any channel.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Config" icon="setting">
		Template settings, AI provider and model, and **Conversation memory**.

		**Clear memory after** decides how long a quiet conversation is remembered: 15 min · 30 min · 1 hour · 4 hours · 24 hours · Never.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Tasks" icon="clock">
		**Scheduled agent tasks**: a prompt that runs on a schedule, like a morning news summary sent to your chat. Pro feature, AI agents only.
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Tools" icon="puzzle">
		Attach [MCP servers](/setup/mcp-tools/) and sign in to them with **Connect**. AI agents only.
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

## Moving to a new phone

**Settings → Backup & Restore** exports bot configuration and schedules to a file, and imports it on the new phone.

<Aside type="caution">
Backups never contain tokens, webhook secrets or API keys. After importing, re-enter them before starting each bot.
</Aside>

Deleting a bot permanently removes the bot, its logs, and its stored secrets.
