Chat is one conversation inside the app. Type a message and it goes to the Assistant, to a bot you address with `@`, or to a command you run with `/`. Chatting here needs no messenger app.

## Open Chat

On the Home screen, tap the **Chat** button. It is the lower of the two round buttons at the bottom-right; the one above it adds a new bot. You can also open Chat from a bot's Bot Detail with **Chat in the app**, with that bot already addressed.

## Just type: the Assistant

{/* ASSISTANT:start */}
No bot, key or setup is needed. The Assistant works even with zero bots, for free and Pro users alike. A plain message with no `@` and no `/` goes to the Assistant, and its replies are tagged **Assistant**.

The Assistant is a text conversation only. It has no tools, Knowledge or channels, and it cannot create or change bots.

For English messages, when the on-device model is downloaded, the Assistant answers on your phone first. For other languages, the cloud backup may answer first, within a daily limit, and an on-device reply then comes with a note that it may be weaker. The cloud backup can also step in when the on-device model is not ready. When neither is available, the app offers to download the on-device model. You can turn the cloud backup off in **Settings**.
{/* ASSISTANT:end */}

## Talk to a bot with @

The `@` list shows your Python bots and your AI bots that are set up. Type `@` and a bot name anywhere in your message, even mid-sentence, and pick the bot from the suggestion list. A **To: bot** chip appears above the message box. Follow-ups keep going to that bot until you clear the chip with ✕. The next plain message after clearing goes to the Assistant.

Picking a Python bot opens its command list. AI bots in Chat need Pro (free users see them with a lock), and they need their provider, key and model set up first. If you address an AI bot that isn't set up, the app shows a **Set up this bot** card and keeps your message.

## Run a command with /

Type `/command arguments` to run a Python bot's command. The command list is grouped by bot. If the same command exists on two bots, the app asks **Which bot?**.

You don't need a bot to try commands. `/price`, `/weather`, `/summary` and `/rss` run straight from their templates, labelled **Runs on** and the template name (for example **Runs on Crypto Price**). Nothing is created or saved. `/summary` needs an AI provider key set up in the app. If one of your bots has the same command, your bot answers instead.

Python bots and template commands work in Chat for free users.

You can also tap **+** and pick **Mention an AI bot** or **Run a bot command** to start a message with `@` or `/`.

## Attachments

Tap the **+** button to add photos from your library, a photo from the camera (no permission prompt) or files. You can attach up to 4 per message.

Only AI bots whose model supports attachments accept them. Python bots do not. If the bot you're sending to can't take them, the app tells you before sending.

## Free vs Pro in Chat

- **Assistant:** free and Pro.
- **Python bots:** free and Pro.
- **AI bots:** Pro.

## History, new conversation and Stop

History is kept when you leave the screen. **New conversation** clears the whole timeline and the memory of every AI bot and of the Assistant.

While a bot is replying, **Stop** replaces Send. It ends the wait and marks the messages **Not answered**, with **Retry**.
